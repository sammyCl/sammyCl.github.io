Phoenixes

Phoenixes are the only creatures in the bestiary that die on a fixed schedule, and what each one does with that certainty sets it apart from the rest of its kind. Sun phoenixes burn at their peak and lead in the open. Ash phoenixes linger in what's left after a burning and tend to it. Embers are young phoenixes still working through their first cycle, not yet sure what they'll become. Eclipse phoenixes bank their fire low and show almost nothing until the moment they choose to.
