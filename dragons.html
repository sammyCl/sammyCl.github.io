Dragons

Dragons are the oldest and longest-lived of the named creatures, and the most varied: what a dragon values, and what it will do to protect it, changes almost entirely by kind. Silver dragons keep watch. Green dragons claim territory. Red dragons take what they want. Gold dragons keep score. Meeting one without knowing which kind you are dealing with is the single most common way an otherwise careful traveler does not come home.
