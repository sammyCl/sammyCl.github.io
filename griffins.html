Griffins

Griffins are creatures of altitude, and the four kinds keep almost nothing in common except the sky itself: each has claimed a different slice of it and built a whole way of life around holding that slice. Mountain griffins nest on a single peak for generations and rarely leave it. Storm griffins ride the front of a coming storm rather than shelter from it. Sand griffins range the open desert on thermals rising off bare rock. Cloud griffins keep to altitudes where few others can follow, and are rarely seen from the ground at all. Knowing which sky you are standing under is the first thing to learn before meeting one.
