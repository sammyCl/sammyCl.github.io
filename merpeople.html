Merpeople

Merpeople are less a single people than four communities that happen to share a body and a home in the water, and what looks like one creature from the shore turns out to be four very different neighbors up close. Reef merfolk build and trade in the shallows, sociable and territorial about their gardens. Abyssal merfolk keep to lightless depths and rarely surface at all. Siren-kin trade in voice, and in wanting. River merfolk claim a single stretch of water and hold it like a border. Assuming a merperson wants what the last one wanted is the fastest way to learn just how different they are.
